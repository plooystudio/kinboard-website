Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how 2545002 AB Ltd., operating as Kinboard (“Kinboard,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, stores, and shares your personal information when you use our platform at kinboard.ca (the “Platform”).

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly and transparently. By using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy should be read alongside our Terms and Conditions, available at kinboard.ca/terms.

2. Who We Are

2545002 AB Ltd., operating as Kinboard, is a software company incorporated in Alberta, Canada. We are the data controller responsible for your personal information collected through the Platform.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us at:

2545002 AB Ltd., operating as Kinboard
Whitecourt, Alberta, Canada
hello@kinboard.ca

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use the Platform, you may provide us with the following types of personal information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password when you create your account
  • Payment information — your billing details processed through Stripe. We never store your full payment card details — these are handled directly and securely by Stripe
  • Family and household information — family member names, profile photos, assigned colours, roles, and any other household information you enter into the Platform
  • Household content — chore assignments, habit stacks, meal plans, grocery lists, calendar events, star rewards, announcements, and any other data you create within the Platform
  • Communications — messages you send to our support team

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Platform, we automatically collect certain technical information including:

  • Usage data — which features you use, how often you access the Platform, pages visited, and actions taken
  • Device and browser information — your IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Session data — login times, session duration, and authentication activity
  • Error and performance data — technical errors and platform performance metrics used to improve the Platform

3.3 Information from Third Parties

We receive limited information from the third-party services we use to operate the Platform:

  • Stripe — when you make a purchase, Stripe confirms your payment and provides us with your email address, billing country, and subscription status. Stripe does not share your full card details with us
  • Google — if you choose to connect Google Calendar, we receive read-only access to the calendar events of connected family members. This connection is optional and can be disconnected at any time in Settings

4. How We Use Your Information

4.1 To Provide and Operate the Platform

  • Create and manage your household account
  • Process your subscription payments
  • Store and retrieve your family’s chores, habits, meals, grocery lists, calendar events, and all other household data
  • Enforce subscription access
  • Send transactional emails including password resets, billing notifications, and account notices

4.2 To Improve and Develop the Platform

  • Analyse usage patterns to understand how features are being used
  • Identify and fix bugs and technical issues
  • Develop new features and improve existing ones
  • Monitor platform performance and security

4.3 To Communicate With You

  • Respond to your support requests and questions
  • Send important notices about your account, subscription, or changes to our Terms or Privacy Policy
  • Send platform updates and feature announcements (you may opt out of non-essential communications at any time)

4.4 To Comply With Legal Obligations

  • Maintain financial records as required by Canadian law
  • Respond to lawful requests from government or regulatory authorities
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions and protect against fraud and abuse

5. Your Household Data

All family and household data you enter into the Platform belongs to you. We use this information exclusively to provide the Platform’s services to your household. Specifically:

  • We do not use your household data to train any systems or models
  • We do not share your household data with other Platform users
  • Each household account is maintained in strict isolation — your data is never visible to or accessible by other Kinboard households
  • Family member profile photos are stored securely on our servers and served only to authenticated sessions belonging to your household

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your personal information only in the following limited circumstances:

6.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We share your information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the Platform, under contractual obligations to protect your data:

  • Stripe (stripe.com) — payment processing. Your payment information is handled directly by Stripe under their own privacy policy and PCI-DSS compliance. We receive only your subscription status and billing email from Stripe
  • Google (google.com) — if you choose to connect Google Calendar, your calendar data is accessed read-only through Google’s API under Google’s own privacy policy. This integration is entirely optional
  • Resend (resend.com) — we use Resend to send transactional emails such as password resets, billing notifications and account invitations. Your email address and the message content are used solely to deliver these communications
  • Railway (railway.com) — application and database hosting. Your account and household data are stored in our database, hosted by Railway on servers that may be located outside Canada, including in the United States
  • Cloudflare (cloudflare.com) — file storage. Profile photos and photo-frame pictures that you upload are stored in Cloudflare R2 storage
  • Anthropic (anthropic.com) — optional AI habit coach. If you use the habit coach, the habit text, its two-minute action and any short note you type are sent to Anthropic’s API to write a brief suggestion. We do not send your name, email address or other household data. Anthropic handles that text under its own terms and privacy policy

6.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your personal information if required to do so by law, court order, or government authority, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Kinboard, our users, or others.

6.3 Business Transfers

If 2545002 AB Ltd. is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you by email and/or a prominent notice on the Platform before your personal information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6.4 With Your Consent

We may share your information in other ways if you have given us explicit consent to do so.

7. Data Storage and Security

7.1 Where Your Data is Stored

Your data is stored with the service providers described in Section 6.1: our application and database are hosted by Railway, and uploaded photos are stored with Cloudflare. As a Canadian-based business we are subject to Canadian privacy law regarding how we handle your personal information.

By using the Platform you acknowledge that your data may be processed on servers located outside of Canada, including in the United States, in connection with the third-party services described in this Privacy Policy. Where such transfers occur we rely on the contractual and legal frameworks established by those third-party providers.

7.2 Security Measures

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse. These measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission using HTTPS/TLS for all Platform communications
  • Password hashing — we never store your password in plain text
  • Access controls — household data is isolated per account and cannot be accessed by other households
  • Session management — authenticated sessions are managed using signed tokens
  • Payment security — all payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified

No system is completely secure. While we take reasonable precautions we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information. If you become aware of any unauthorized access to your account please contact us immediately at hello@kinboard.ca.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter as required for legal, accounting, and fraud prevention purposes.

Specifically:

  • Active account data — retained for the duration of your subscription and for 90 days after account deactivation, after which it is deleted or anonymised
  • Payment and billing records — retained for a minimum of 7 years as required by Canadian tax and accounting law
  • Support communications — retained for up to 2 years
  • Usage and technical logs — retained for up to 12 months

If you request deletion of your account and personal data we will process your request in accordance with Section 10 of this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The Platform uses essential cookies and similar technologies to operate correctly. These include:

  • Session cookies — to keep you logged in during your session
  • Authentication tokens — to verify your identity and maintain your secure session

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking cookies, or behavioural advertising on the Platform. We do not share your data with advertising networks.

The landing page at kinboard.ca may use basic analytics to understand visitor traffic. If analytics are implemented they will be used only in aggregate form and will not be used to identify individual visitors for advertising purposes.

10. Your Privacy Rights

As a user of Kinboard and as a resident of Canada or other applicable jurisdictions, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

10.1 Right to Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you. To make an access request contact us at hello@kinboard.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

10.2 Right to Correction

You have the right to request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information we hold about you. Most of your account information can be updated directly within your account settings.

10.3 Right to Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal information. You may delete your account directly through Settings → Account → Delete Account, or contact us at hello@kinboard.ca with the subject line “Data Deletion Request.” We will confirm what data we can delete and what, if any, we are required to retain for legal or accounting purposes. We will process deletion requests within 30 days. Please note that deleting your account will result in permanent loss of all your household data including family profiles, chores, habits, meal plans, and all other Platform content. This cannot be undone.

10.4 Right to Data Export

You may export a full backup of your household data at any time through Settings → Data Backup & Restore → Export Backup. This provides a complete JSON file of all your household data that you can retain for your own records.

10.5 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where we rely on your consent to process your personal information, including optional Google Calendar integration, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time through your account settings or by contacting us at hello@kinboard.ca.

10.6 Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we have handled your personal information in a manner that does not comply with applicable privacy law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca, or with the Alberta Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner at oipc.ab.ca.

11. Google Calendar Integration

If you choose to connect Google Calendar to Kinboard, the following applies:

  • We request read-only access to the Google Calendar accounts of family members you choose to connect
  • Calendar events are retrieved and displayed within the Kinboard family calendar view
  • We never create, modify or delete events in your Google Calendar. Habit reminders are sent by Kinboard itself.
  • Kinboard’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You may disconnect Google Calendar at any time through Settings → Google Calendar Sync
  • Google’s use of your data through this integration is subject to Google’s own Privacy Policy at google.com/privacy

12. Children’s Privacy

Kinboard is designed as a family platform and is intended to be managed by adults on behalf of their household. The account holder must be at least 18 years of age. Family member profiles for children may be created within the Platform by the adult account holder. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. All data relating to child family members is entered and controlled by the adult account holder.

If you believe a minor has independently created an account without adult supervision please contact us at hello@kinboard.ca.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you use in connection with the Platform.
